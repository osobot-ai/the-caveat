# The PRL Digital Asset Treasury

OsoFund holds **1,886,502 OYSTER**. We deployed **$3,000** at a blended price of **$0.0015902**, equivalent to an entry market cap of approximately **$1.59 million**.

We are publishing this after taking the position. That conflict is the point: this is research with skin in the game, not neutral commentary.

## OYSTER is building the PRL DAT

Michael Saylor’s Strategy made the digital asset treasury investable: accumulate a scarce network asset, expose the balance publicly, and build a repeatable engine for acquiring more of it.

OYSTER is attempting a crypto-native version for Pearl.

Its trading activity creates creator-side fees. Under the project’s treasury commitment, those fees acquire native PRL into a public Pearl address. The market can therefore evaluate not only the PRL held today, but whether OYSTER can keep turning its own velocity into a growing reserve.

**Strategy turns capital-market velocity into Bitcoin. OYSTER turns trading velocity into Pearl.**

That is why we think of OYSTER as a fee-native PRL digital asset treasury—a community DAT, not a conventional company.

## The Strategy parallel

Strategy uses equity, convertibles, preferred securities, and operating cash to acquire Bitcoin. Investors track its Bitcoin balance, Bitcoin per share, and whether new financing acquires more Bitcoin on an accretive basis.

OYSTER replaces corporate capital markets with token activity:

1. Attention creates OYSTER trading volume.
2. Trading volume creates creator-side fees.
3. Those fees finance PRL purchases.
4. PRL enters a public treasury.
5. A growing treasury can strengthen the story, attract attention, and create more volume.

At the stated fee split, approximately **0.7% of OYSTER volume** is available for PRL accumulation. Every $1 million of volume can therefore create roughly **$7,000 of purchasing power** for the treasury.

The opening balance is not the ceiling. **The acquisition engine is the asset.**

## Where the analogy ends

The distinction is essential. Strategy is a public company. Its shareholders own equity. OYSTER is a token whose holders do not currently have a contractual redemption, ownership, or governance claim on the PRL reserve.

“PRL DAT” describes the economic thesis, not a legal structure. It is better understood as an early, operator-directed, fee-funded community treasury. There is no debt or equity capital-markets engine, and there is no guarantee that treasury value transmits into OYSTER price.

That makes OYSTER riskier than a conventional DAT—but it also creates the asymmetry we are underwriting.

## The machine

Pearl replaces Bitcoin-style hash grinding with the operation that dominates modern AI: matrix multiplication.

Its proof-of-useful-work protocol allows a GPU to perform an inference or training workload and use the same multiplication to compete for PRL block rewards. Selected work is verified with compact cryptographic proofs without publishing model weights, prompts, or full matrices.

The base layer is deliberately conservative: a UTXO chain derived from btcd, Taproot-style addresses, a full node, SPV client, ZK proving system, GPU miner, and a maximum supply of 2.1 billion PRL. Mainnet has produced blocks since April 27, 2026.

Bitcoin monetized the machine that hashes. Pearl is trying to monetize the machine that thinks.

## The proof

Pearl already has what most proof-of-useful-work networks never reach: a real AI product with an external operator.

Together AI launched an exclusive Pearl-powered Gemma endpoint priced more than 25% below its standard endpoint. The discount is funded by the expected value of PRL produced during the same forward pass. One GPU cycle can create two outputs: paid inference and a monetary asset.

The current network uses an integer-based scheme with founder-reported overhead of roughly 10%–15%. The next Pearl-GEMM design targets native FP8 workloads on Hopper and Blackwell GPUs with founder-reported 0%–3% end-to-end overhead.

The catalyst is not another whitepaper. It is the distance between one production endpoint and an inference platform.

## The treasury vehicle

Pearl’s own open-source wallet daemon is named **Oyster**. The token did not need to invent the metaphor; the Pearl stack already wrote it into the lore.

OYSTER adds a liquid, EVM-native asset designed to turn activity into PRL accumulation. Its Pons V2 pool charges a 1% hook fee. Pons retains 30% of that fee; the creator-side 70%—approximately 0.7% of volume—is available for PRL purchases under the treasury commitment.

The public treasury has received its first 2,500 PRL. That is useful as activation evidence: the loop has executed once. It is not the center of the valuation case. The thesis depends on repetition, scale, and PRL appreciation.

## DAT economics: quantity × price

The treasury can compound on two axes:

- OYSTER volume can increase the quantity of PRL accumulated.
- Pearl adoption can increase the value of every PRL already accumulated.

At the publication PRL price of approximately $1.3453:

- 10,000 PRL is worth approximately $13,453.
- At a 10× PRL price, the same 10,000 PRL is worth approximately $134,530.
- At a 100× PRL price, the same 10,000 PRL is worth approximately $1.345 million.

That sensitivity is mechanical for the treasury. OYSTER appreciation is not. A growing reserve can strengthen the narrative and improve treasury value per token, but holders have no contractual claim and the market can assign any premium or discount it chooses.

## What breaks this open

1. **A repeatable purchase cadence.** Recurring fee claims and PRL purchases prove this is an operating DAT rather than a one-post treasury story.
2. **PRL per OYSTER rises.** A consistently improving reserve-per-token metric would be the crypto-native analogue to a DAT growing assets per share.
3. **Pearl-GEMM reaches mainnet.** Native FP8 support moves useful-work mining closer to the workloads frontier inference actually uses.
4. **Together expands the product.** More Pearl-powered endpoints turn PRL into a recurring subsidy for customer-funded inference.
5. **Pearl recognizes the culture.** Adoption by Pearl miners, builders, or official ecosystem accounts would collapse the distance between unofficial meme and network-native cultural asset.
6. **EVM distribution compounds.** Wallets, venues, listings, and Robinhood Chain access can distribute OYSTER while continuously pointing attention back to Pearl.

## What can prove us wrong

The project is days old. Its launch distribution was highly concentrated. PRL purchases are operator-directed rather than enforced by the token contract. The reported OTC payment terms are not independently proven by the destination-chain receipt. Both assets trade in volatile, thin-liquidity markets.

The cleanest version requires a joined, auditable receipt chain: every creator-fee claim paired with its source-chain payment and destination-chain PRL receipt. If purchases stop, PRL per OYSTER fails to rise, or the treasury remains a marketing object instead of a repeatable strategy, the DAT thesis fails.
