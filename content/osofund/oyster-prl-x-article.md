Bitcoin turns energy into money. Pearl turns AI computation into money. **OYSTER turns attention into Pearl.**

That loop is no longer theoretical.

The trade is not that OYSTER becomes Pearl. The trade is that Pearl gets a collector.

## The machine

Pearl replaces Bitcoin-style hash grinding with useful matrix multiplication.

Its proof-of-useful-work protocol allows a GPU to perform an inference or training workload and use the same multiplication to compete for PRL block rewards. Selected work is verified with compact cryptographic proofs without publishing model weights, prompts, or full matrices.

The base layer is deliberately conservative: a UTXO chain derived from btcd, Taproot-style addresses, a full node, SPV client, ZK proving system, GPU miner, and a maximum supply of 2.1 billion PRL. Mainnet has produced blocks since April 27, 2026.

Bitcoin monetized the machine that hashes. Pearl is trying to monetize the machine that thinks.

## The proof

Pearl already has what most proof-of-useful-work networks never reach: a real AI product with an external operator.

Together AI launched an exclusive Pearl-powered Gemma endpoint priced more than 25% below its standard endpoint. The discount is funded by the expected value of PRL produced during the same forward pass. One GPU cycle can create two outputs: paid inference and a monetary asset.

The current network uses an integer-based scheme with founder-reported overhead of roughly 10%–15%. The next Pearl-GEMM design targets native FP8 workloads on Hopper and Blackwell GPUs with founder-reported 0%–3% end-to-end overhead, including near-zero on DeepSeek V4 Flash.

The catalyst is not another whitepaper. It is the distance between one production endpoint and an inference platform.

## The collector

Pearl's own open-source wallet daemon is named **Oyster**. The token did not need to invent the metaphor; the Pearl stack already wrote it into the lore.

OYSTER adds a liquid, EVM-native asset that turns activity into PRL accumulation. Its Pons V2 pool charges a 1% hook fee. Pons retains 30% of that fee; the creator-side 70%—approximately **0.7% of trading volume**—is available for PRL purchases under the treasury commitment.

At that rate:

- $1 million of OYSTER volume creates approximately $7,000 for PRL accumulation.
- At the publication PRL price of about $1.345, that would acquire approximately 5,200 PRL.
- Every PRL receipt and reserve balance can be observed on Pearl mainnet.

The loop is simple:

**OYSTER attention → trading volume → fees → PRL purchases → visible reserve → stronger lore → more attention.**

PRL is the engine. OYSTER is the collector sitting beside it.

## What breaks this open

1. **Reserve repetition.** The next fee claim becomes the next PRL receipt. A cadence proves OYSTER is an operating collector, not a one-post treasury story.
2. **The first 10,000 PRL.** A five-figure reserve makes PRL per OYSTER, accumulation rate, and reserve growth easy to track.
3. **Pearl-GEMM reaches mainnet.** Native FP8 support moves useful-work mining closer to the workloads frontier inference actually uses.
4. **Together expands the product.** More Pearl-powered endpoints turn PRL into a recurring subsidy for customer-funded inference.
5. **Pearl recognizes the culture.** Adoption by Pearl miners, builders, or official ecosystem accounts would collapse the distance between unofficial meme and network-native cultural asset.
6. **EVM distribution compounds.** OYSTER can travel through EVM wallets, liquidity venues, and Robinhood Chain distribution while continuously pointing attention back to Pearl.

## What can prove us wrong

OYSTER holders do not currently have a contractual redemption, ownership, or governance claim on the PRL reserve. The reserve is a fee-funded treasury—not token backing.

The project is days old. Its launch distribution was highly concentrated. PRL purchases are operator-directed rather than enforced by the token contract. The OTC payment and reported ETH balance are not yet joined to public onchain receipts. Both assets trade in volatile, thin-liquidity markets.

The cleanest version of the thesis requires repeated PRL purchases, joined fee-to-reserve receipts, and a public cadence that outlives the launch narrative. If the reserve stops growing, the collector story stops working.
